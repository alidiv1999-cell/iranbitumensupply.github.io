Common Mistakes Buyers Make When Importing Bitumen

Many import challenges come from incomplete planning rather than market price alone.

Frequent mistakes include weak quality verification, unsuitable packaging selection, and unrealistic timeline assumptions around port and customs handling.

A robust pre-shipment checklist and clear contract clauses reduce claims, demurrage exposure, and project delays.
