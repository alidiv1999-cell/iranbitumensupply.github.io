How to Choose a Reliable Bitumen Supplier for International Projects

Choosing the right bitumen supplier affects product quality, delivery reliability, and total project risk.

Start by verifying refinery origin and traceability. Refinery-direct supply usually improves consistency and reduces off-spec disputes.

Then confirm documentation readiness (COA, MSDS, packing list, bill of lading) and export experience with your destination ports and Incoterms model.
