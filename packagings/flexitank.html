Flexitank Packaging Option

Flexitank systems are installed inside standard shipping containers to transport liquid bitumen and related products efficiently and safely. This method makes excellent use of container space, allowing higher payload capacity per shipment compared with traditional packaging options. Flexitanks are especially useful for export operations that require clean handling, reduced packaging waste, and optimized shipping efficiency. With proper heating, pumping, and discharge procedures, this solution supports smooth unloading at the destination and reliable product transfer for industrial buyers.

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Higher container utilization: Flexitanks maximize payload per container, which improves shipping efficiency and cost effectiveness.
  • ✔ Cleaner discharge process: The system reduces residue, packaging waste, and manual handling compared with drum-based options.
  • ✔ Efficient for liquid transport: It is well suited for liquid bitumen and similar products that need controlled heating and pumping during discharge.
