Flexitank for liquid container shipment.

Flexitank Packaging Option

Flexitank systems are installed inside standard shipping containers to carry liquid bitumen and related products. This method increases payload utilization per container and supports cleaner discharge operations when handled with proper heating and pumping procedures.

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Secure handling and transport planning
  • ✔ Suitable for international export logistics
  • ✔ Can be aligned with client unloading capabilities
