Jumbo Bags Packaging Option

Jumbo bags are flexible intermediate bulk containers (FIBCs) typically used for medium-volume bitumen orders. They help optimize loading efficiency, reduce packaging material compared with drums, and are practical for projects with controlled unloading facilities.

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Secure handling and transport planning
  • ✔ Suitable for international export logistics
  • ✔ Can be aligned with client unloading capabilities
