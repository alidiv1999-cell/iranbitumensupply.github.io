Steel Drums Packaging Option

Steel drums are one of the most common and reliable packaging options for bitumen exports, especially for smaller to medium-sized shipments. They are strong, durable, and designed to protect bitumen during handling, storage, and international transportation. Because steel drums are stackable and easy to load, they are suitable for road transport, sea freight, and multimodal shipping routes. Depending on the product grade and customer requirements, common drum net weights include 150 kg, 180 kg, and 220 kg.

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Excellent product protection: Steel drums help preserve bitumen quality by shielding it from contamination, leakage, and external damage during transit.
  • ✔ Easy stacking and handling: Their rigid structure makes them convenient for warehouse storage, container loading, and efficient transport planning.
  • ✔ Versatile for many shipping routes: Steel drums are suitable for road, sea, and multimodal shipments, making them a flexible option for different export destinations.
