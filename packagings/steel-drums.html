Steel Drums Packaging Option

Steel drums are widely used for smaller export lots of bitumen. They are durable, stackable, and suitable for road, sea, and multimodal transport. Common drum sizes include 150 kg, 180 kg, and 220 kg net weight depending on product type and customer preference.
