Bulk Shipment Packaging Option

Bulk shipment is the preferred packaging and logistics method for large-volume bitumen exports and long-term supply contracts. In this method, bitumen is loaded directly into insulated tankers or specialized vessels and transported without intermediate packaging, making it highly efficient for industrial-scale deliveries. At the destination, the product is discharged into terminals equipped with storage tanks, heating systems, and pumping units to ensure safe handling and continuous supply for refineries, contractors, and large infrastructure projects.

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Lower handling cost: Bulk shipment reduces the need for drums, bags, and manual packaging, which helps lower total logistics costs.
  • ✔ Faster loading and unloading: Direct transfer into tanks or vessels speeds up the shipping process and improves delivery efficiency.
  • ✔ Best for high-volume contracts: It is ideal for buyers with continuous demand, making it especially suitable for long-term industrial supply agreements.
