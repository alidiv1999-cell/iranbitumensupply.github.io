Iran–Pakistan Overland Bitumen: Taftan & Gabd-Rimdan Trucking Routes

Pakistan is, by volume, the single largest destination for Iranian bitumen exports — the overland border relationship, not just shipping lanes, defines this trade. Iranian bitumen moves into Pakistan by truck through the Taftan and Gabd-Rimdan crossings in Balochistan, a land bridge that operates independently of Gulf sea lanes and has become an increasingly important supplement to maritime routing whenever regional shipping conditions in the Strait of Hormuz tighten.

Why overland trucking dominates this lane

Unlike every other market on this list, Pakistan doesn't need a sea leg at all. Bitumen produced at Iranian refineries can be trucked directly across the border and on to Quetta, Karachi-linked distribution networks, and national highway contractors without ever touching a port. That cuts out freight, port handling, and transshipment costs that weigh down every maritime alternative, and it also means this route keeps functioning reliably even during periods when Gulf shipping is under pressure — which is exactly why Iran supplies the overwhelming majority of Pakistan's bitumen imports.

What Pakistan buys

  • Bulk tanker truck shipments of Bitumen 60/70 and 80/100 for National Highway Authority and provincial road projects
  • Drummed bitumen for smaller contractors and remote up-country sites without bulk storage
  • Consistent, high-frequency loads rather than one-off spot cargoes, given the direct land route

Logistics reality

The Gabd-Rimdan and Taftan crossings both run through Balochistan via established truck corridors (Turbat, Panjgur, Khuzdar, Quetta, Dalbandin), with essential commodities including bitumen processed through Pakistan Customs' expedited clearance channels. We quote FOB ex-refinery/border for this route, since overland pricing is a matter of trucking cost rather than vessel freight. Standard export documentation still applies, and we'll confirm current border-crossing status and clearance procedures at the time of your inquiry, since these are periodically updated by Pakistan Customs.
