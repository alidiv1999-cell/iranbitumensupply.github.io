Iran Bitumen Supplier for Turkey: Mersin Gateway to the Mediterranean & Beyond

Turkey is the pivot point where Iranian bitumen exports meet the Mediterranean, European, and wider Eurasian trade — a role reinforced by Turkey's place in the Middle Corridor rail and transit network connecting Central Asia to Europe. Mersin functions as both a genuine Turkish demand center and a launch point for cargo continuing further west, which is why traders routinely quote Mersin alongside Jebel Ali and Bandar Abbas as one of the key reference ports for regional bitumen pricing.

Why Turkey is a gatekeeper market

Turkey's own infrastructure pipeline is substantial, but its real strategic value for exporters is geographic: it's the last stop before the Mediterranean and the natural rail/road junction for the Middle Corridor linking China, Central Asia, the Caucasus, and Europe. A supplier with an established Turkish logistics presence — office, warehousing, or transit arrangements — has a real edge routing product both into Turkey and onward.

What Turkey buys

  • Bulk and drummed Bitumen 60/70/80/100 for domestic road and industrial projects
  • Cargo positioned for onward Mediterranean and European re-export via established maritime transit offices
  • Oxidized bitumen grades for roofing, waterproofing, and industrial coating demand

Logistics reality

Mersin is a standard reference point in weekly regional bitumen pricing alongside Jebel Ali and Bandar Abbas. We quote FOB as standard; CFR Mersin is available for buyers who want a fixed landed number for onward Mediterranean or European distribution. Full export documentation and SGS/Bureau Veritas inspection are standard practice for Turkish customs clearance.
