Iran Bitumen Supplier for Indonesia: Bulk Cargo for Jakarta & the National Megaproject Pipeline

Indonesia's archipelago geography and megaproject-driven construction agenda — new capital city development, national toll road expansion, and inter-island connectivity projects — make it one of the largest and most structurally durable bitumen import markets in Southeast Asia, with Jakarta as the standard entry point.

Why Indonesia's demand is structurally large, not cyclical

Indonesia's infrastructure agenda spans toll roads, the new capital (Nusantara) build-out, and inter-island transport links across a geography that inherently multiplies road-construction material needs compared with more compact markets. That combination of scale and multi-year project pipelines gives Indonesia unusually durable demand relative to markets more exposed to a single budget or political cycle.

What Indonesia buys

  • Bulk and container cargoes of Bitumen 60/70 for toll road and national infrastructure projects
  • Drummed bitumen for outer-island and remote project sites without port-adjacent bulk storage
  • Growing demand for polymer-modified and warm-mix-compatible grades as Indonesian standards evolve

Logistics reality

We quote FOB Bandar Abbas as standard; CFR Jakarta is available on request for buyers who want a fixed landed price. Jakarta sits within the regional Southeast Asian pricing basket alongside Vietnam and the Philippines. Given the archipelago's geography, buyers frequently need onward inter-island transport arranged after port clearance — a logistics detail worth addressing directly with contractors sourcing for outer-island projects.
