Why Armenia matters

Armenia is Iran's direct northern land gateway into the South Caucasus — a border relationship, similar in spirit to the Pakistan corridor, where bitumen can move overland rather than by sea. With regional interest in Caucasus transport connectivity continuing to build, Armenia's role as a transit and consumption market is one worth establishing early rather than waiting on.

What Armenia buys

  • Bulk and drummed bitumen for domestic road construction and rehabilitation projects
  • Product positioned for potential onward movement into the broader Caucasus/Middle Corridor trade as regional transport links develop

Logistics reality

We quote FOB as standard for this lane, with CFR available on request where appropriate. Standard export documentation, inspection certificates, and current routing conditions are confirmed at the time of inquiry so buyers can plan lead times with confidence.
