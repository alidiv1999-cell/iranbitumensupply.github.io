Why Turkey matters

Turkey is the pivot point where Iranian bitumen exports meet the Mediterranean, European, and wider Eurasian trade — a role reinforced by Turkey's place in the Middle Corridor rail and transit network connecting Central Asia to Europe. Mersin functions as both a genuine Turkish demand center and a launch point for cargo continuing further west, which is why traders routinely quote Mersin alongside Jebel Ali and Bandar Abbas as one of the key reference ports for regional bitumen pricing.

What Turkey buys

  • Bulk and drummed Bitumen 60/70/80/100 for domestic road and industrial projects
  • Cargo positioned for onward Mediterranean and European re-export via established maritime transit offices
  • Oxidized bitumen grades for roofing, waterproofing, and industrial coating demand
