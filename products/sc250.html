Cutback Bitumen SC‑250 – General Description │ Iran Bitumen Supply Int │ IBSI

Cutback Bitumen SC‑250 is a slow‑curing cutback asphalt produced by blending high‑quality penetration‑grade bitumen with low‑volatility petroleum distillates, typically gas oil or similar heavy oils. These solvents reduce the viscosity of the bitumen, allowing it to be applied at ambient temperatures while providing a much slower evaporation rate compared to rapid‑curing (RC) or medium‑curing (MC) grades. Slow‑curing cutbacks like SC‑250 are often referred to as road oils due to their heavier composition and extended curing time. They behave similarly to residual refinery products such as bunker fuel oils. SC‑250 is designed for applications where deep penetration, long working time, and strong binding of granular surfaces are required. At Iran Bitumen Supply Int │ IBSI, our SC‑250 is manufactured from premium vacuum residue feedstock and blended with carefully selected low‑volatility solvents to ensure consistent curing behavior. The product complies with international standards for slow‑curing cutback petroleum asphalts and is suitable for a wide range of road construction and maintenance applications.

Understanding How SC‑250 Works

Cutback asphalt is produced by adding petroleum solvents to bitumen to make it liquid at ambient temperatures. In SC‑250, the solvent used is a less volatile distillate, which evaporates slowly. This slow evaporation allows the bitumen to remain workable for a longer period, making SC‑250 ideal for applications requiring extended curing time and deeper penetration into granular surfaces. As the solvent gradually evaporates, the bitumen returns to its original viscosity and hardness. When properly formulated, SC‑250 provides excellent adhesion, long‑term durability, and strong waterproofing performance.

Applications of Cutback Bitumen SC‑250

Cutback Bitumen SC‑250 is widely used in road construction and maintenance, particularly in prime coats, patching materials, and stockpile asphalt mixes. Its slow curing properties make it ideal for stabilizing granular base courses before applying hot mix asphalt. SC‑250 penetrates deeply into the surface, fills capillary voids, and binds loose particles to create a strong, waterproof foundation. SC‑250 is also used in cold‑applied asphalt treatments, where heating equipment is not available or practical. Its ability to wet aggregates effectively makes it suitable for surface treatments, seal coats, and waterproofing applications. It is commonly applied to protect surfaces, bond mineral particles, and prepare non‑asphalt layers for further construction. Because SC‑250 does not require heating, it is especially useful in remote areas, rural road projects, and maintenance operations where simplicity and ease of application are essential.

Safety Guidelines for Handling SC‑250

SC‑250 contains petroleum solvents and must be handled with care. It should be transported, stored, and applied at the lowest practical temperature. All ignition sources must be eliminated during use due to the flammable nature of the solvents. Direct inhalation of vapors and skin contact should be avoided, and appropriate personal protective equipment must be worn at all times. The product and any washings must never be allowed to enter stormwater or sewer systems.
