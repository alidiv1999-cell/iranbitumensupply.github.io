What Is Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen (CRMB 50)? – Iran Bitumen Supply Int │ IBSI

Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen CRMB 50 is a high‑performance, elastomeric bitumen produced by blending premium paving‑grade bitumen with finely ground crumb rubber derived from recycled automobile tires. This modification significantly enhances the elasticity, resilience, and temperature resistance of the binder, making CRMB 50 one of the most durable and sustainable materials used in modern road construction. At Iran Bitumen Supply Int │ IBSI, CRMB 50 is engineered to deliver superior performance in regions exposed to heavy traffic loads, high pavement stress, and large temperature variations. The addition of rubber particles improves the binder’s flexibility at low temperatures and its resistance to deformation at high temperatures, resulting in pavements that last longer, resist cracking, and maintain structural integrity under demanding conditions. CRMB 50 is widely used as an advanced alternative to conventional bitumen in areas where roads must withstand continuous loading, rutting, and fatigue. Its enhanced elasticity and improved adhesion to aggregates make it ideal for high‑performance asphalt mixes.

Applications of Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen CRMB 50

CRMB 50 is primarily used in high‑stress road construction, where long‑term durability and resistance to cracking are essential. It is commonly applied in the construction of highways, expressways, and industrial pavements that experience heavy truck traffic. The rubber modification improves the binder’s ability to absorb shock and vibration, making it suitable for regions with significant temperature fluctuations or challenging terrain. In addition to road construction, CRMB 50 is used in pavement rehabilitation, where it helps restore flexibility and extend the lifespan of aged asphalt surfaces. It is also applied in bridge decks, junctions, intersections, and roundabouts, where pavements are subjected to constant braking and turning forces. The enhanced elasticity of CRMB 50 reduces the risk of reflective cracking and improves overall pavement performance.

Properties of Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen CRMB 50

CRMB 50 is characterized by its improved elasticity, higher softening point, and enhanced resistance to deformation. The presence of crumb rubber increases the binder’s viscosity, allowing it to maintain stability under high temperatures. Its penetration value is lower than conventional bitumen, indicating a harder and more resilient binder suitable for heavy‑duty applications. The softening point of CRMB 50 is significantly higher than standard paving‑grade bitumen, ensuring that pavements remain stable even in hot climates. Its elastic recovery is also superior, allowing the binder to stretch and return to its original shape without cracking. These properties make CRMB 50 an excellent choice for pavements exposed to repeated loading and thermal stress.

At Iran Bitumen Supply Int │ IBSI, Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen CRMB 50 is available in multiple packaging formats to meet diverse project requirements. It can be supplied in new steel drums, Bitubags, or bulk shipments depending on the scale of the project. For large infrastructure developments, CRMB 50 is often delivered in bulk bitumen carriers or tanker trucks, ensuring efficient and continuous supply to asphalt plants.
