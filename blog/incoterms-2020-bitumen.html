Understanding Incoterms 2020 in Bitumen Export Contracts

Incoterms 2020 define the obligations of buyers and sellers in international trade contracts.

Under FOB, seller risk ends once cargo is loaded at origin. Under CFR, seller covers freight but not insurance. Under CIF, seller includes insurance to destination port.

Selecting the right Incoterm helps align payment structure, insurance scope, and logistics responsibility before shipment starts.
