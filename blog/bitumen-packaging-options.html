Bitumen Packaging Options Explained: Choosing the Right Packing Method

Packaging affects quality preservation, transport safety, and unloading efficiency in bitumen exports.

Steel drums suit controlled smaller shipments, jumbo bags optimize handling, flexitanks improve container payload, and bulk is ideal for large-volume terminals.

Selecting packing according to route, equipment, and destination infrastructure lowers risk and improves delivery performance.
