Bitumen Grades Explained: Choosing the Right Type for Your Project

Different projects require different bitumen grades depending on climate, traffic load, and application process.

Common categories include penetration grades, viscosity grades, oxidized grades, modified bitumen, and cutback bitumen.

Matching technical grade selection to design conditions improves pavement life, compliance performance, and lifecycle cost control.
